# 0003. Roto leagues

Date: 2026-11-26. Status: accepted (design approved by the owner on issue #14; luck numbers shown to the owner before the build). Method and calibration: `docs/roto-method.md`.

## Decision
1. Roto has no weekly winner. Each team is ranked in every category on season totals; best of N teams earns N points, worst 1; tied teams split the points of the places they cover; most points wins. Lower-is-better categories (TO, PF) use `isLowerCategory`.
2. The engine answers for the rest of the season: expected final roto points, the chance of each finishing place (team check), and for a waiver add or a trade the change in expected points and in the chance of finishing first and in the top few (default 3). There is no weekly matchup, outlook, add limit or daily lineup in roto.
3. Inputs come from the host: `league.format: "roto"`, `league.standings` (each team's name and season totals, `me: true` on the user's own team, every other team's roster) and the user's roster. Percentage and ratio categories take made and attempted counts (ESPN); when only a percentage is shown (Yahoo) banked attempts are estimated from each rostered player's per-game attempts x games played, and the output is labelled `SHOOTING ESTIMATED`.
4. Projection: final total = banked total + sum over players of per-game average x games he is expected to play. Per-game averages are the engine's prior-season estimate blended with this season so far (weight games / (games + 30)). Games left come from the day-by-day NBA schedule; a player's expected share of them is his games so far shrunk toward the league rate for that point in the season (K = 25); a known injury window from the league page removes games. Derived categories are computed from base totals as in the any-category engine.
5. Luck, measured on 2025-26 (`engine/scripts/measure-roto-model.py` generates `engine/src/rotoluck.ts`): one games-played draw per player (spread measured by games left) plus per-game luck on each base stat, `sqrt(a^2 / games per player + b^2)`, with the measured correlation matrix. It shrinks as games run out but a persistent part stays.
6. Limits, stated in every output: no bench or daily lineup limits (every rostered player counts), no games-played caps, no positions, no IL rules, other teams hold their rosters, no playoffs. The games-played share is an assumption, calibrated in the back-test.
7. Nobody has a real roto league to check against, so the back-test uses simulated leagues on 2025-26 (`engine/scripts/roto-backtest.ts`).

## Consequences
Standard-nine, any-category and points results are unchanged (existing tests). The bundle `skills/team-check/scripts/analyze.mjs` includes the roto path. Skills stay read-only and report measured numbers only; Fantasy Value is never shown.
